Empower Academy · Proposed for ADNOC Distribution

Practical skills. Confidence in car care.

Empower Academy, led by Jowana Burgan: a proposed partnership starting with practical education and a better service experience for women at ADNOC Distribution, then growing through evidence and the right partners.

Proposed first entry: lubricants, car care and customer experience. The wider vision includes EmpowerDrive, hands-on STEM and career exploration.

Stage one · ADNOC Distribution

First ask: a service and education pilot

Car Care Confidence Pilot

A small pilot combining women’s practical workshops, accessible content and feedback on the service journey and staff communication. Measure learning and service clarity before extending the partnership.

Proposed output

Customer education

Two expert-supervised sessions for a proposed cohort of up to 24 adult women: maintenance basics, understanding oil requirements through the vehicle manual, and questions to ask before approving repairs.

Proposed output

Service experience

Review one agreed service location, draft plain-language pricing and repair explanations, and hold a staff session on clear, respectful communication. Test recommendations with the company.

Proposed output

Content and follow-up

Four short, technically reviewed learning assets, an optional referral to an approved service channel, and a report on participation, learning, experience and trackable enquiries.

Agree numbers, venue and outputs. An initial delivery window is 6–8 weeks after agreement and resource readiness. Company approval covers branding, channels and technical materials. Sessions build consumer knowledge; they do not confer technician qualifications.

Empower Academy’s original stages, with clear gates for progression

One vision, three stages

  1. Start here

    Service-centre experience

    Customer learning, clearer communication and accessible technical content. Start with a Distribution pilot; review results and cost before repeating.

  2. Later development

    EmpowerDrive

    A confidence-first driving programme through a licensed training partner. Assess audience, curriculum and delivery capacity first; road training requires the relevant approvals.

  3. Future vision

    Connected academy

    Scale hands-on STEM and life-skills workshops and develop a Career Map Navigator prototype supported by human guidance. Assess the physical academy and education and funding pathways through a separate feasibility study.

Stage-one evidence informs further collaboration. Each later stage needs its own owner, specialist partner and budget. Education modules can develop with an appropriate partner while the wider academy is assessed.
