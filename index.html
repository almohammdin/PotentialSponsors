Sponsorship structure that protects platform value

Clear split between platform sponsor, category sponsor and activation partner
Founding Platform Sponsor

Platform sponsor

Price rights after defining scope, exclusivity and delivery. No validated platform-wide rate is available.

Category Sponsor

Category partner

Scope each category around audience, rights and delivery cost. Current Al Rostamani proposal: AED 150k for 6 months or AED 250k for one year.

Activation Partner

Activation partner

A smaller workshop or campaign may use AED 15k–40k as an internal planning range, reviewed after costing.

Recommendation: do not sell the whole app first. Start with a 6-month automotive or car-services category sponsorship, measure performance, then convert into an annual agreement.
5

First pilot plan

A structured route before wider negotiations
1

Select 5 prospects

Al Rostamani, AWR, CAFU, Sukoon and Service My Car.

2

Tailor the message

Each prospect gets a different angle: service, insurance, safety or empowerment.

3

20-minute call

Test initial interest without over-explaining the entire app.

4

Offer one category

Sell “Car Confidence” or “Insurance Confidence”, not the whole platform.

5

Measurable pilot

A 3–6 month pilot: agree attendance, engagement, code and referral targets. Record a baseline and review the results.

Before final pricing: actual audience data, delivery costs, a conversion definition, visibility and exclusivity rights, and content approval. Qualify each organisation separately.
